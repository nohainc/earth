# V5 Territory Domain Contract

Territory is a physical/geographic capacity region of Earth. It is not
inherently a government, Corporation, market, treasury, tax authority, or
political institution.

## Canonical Territory responsibilities

- geographic identity, name, type, and lifecycle status;
- House residency and Corporation location context;
- physical capacity, population, and infrastructure projections;
- environmental and regional conditions;
- future resource modifiers, climate, and geography mechanics.

## Retired social authority

The following are historical compatibility structures only and must not be
used by new V5 runtime code:

- `territory_governance`;
- `territory_rights` and lease payments;
- Territory treasury, Territory tax, or Territory dividend authority;
- Territory commons declarations and player-facing use-right APIs.

The corresponding tables remain readable for audit and migration history. New
economic mutations must use Earth, Corporation, or House authorities and V5
capacity read models.
