# Daily settlement V3

- Status: Canonical

Daily settlement follows the domain model:

- EARTH settles global taxes and global systems.
- Corporations settle local taxes, budgets, public infrastructure funding, and
  local political effects.
- Territories refresh geographic capacity and service projections.
- Humans and Houses receive downstream maintenance and economic settlement.

The settlement registry contains no City service projection, City dynamics, or
City tax phase. `territory_capacity_state` is rebuilt from Territory buildings,
catalog effects, ownership, and active House affiliations at day close.
