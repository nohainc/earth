# Client hierarchy V3

- Status: Canonical

The client hierarchy is EARTH → Corporation → Territory, with House and Human
identity shown beneath the Corporation context. Territory is rendered as a
Corporation subsection and exposes geographic capacity/infrastructure rather
than a separate political screen.

Building models use `territory_id`; construction sends `territoryId`.
Corporation fiscal and Territory APIs are the client authorities for local
budgets, taxes, capacity, and services.
